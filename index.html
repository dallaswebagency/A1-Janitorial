03 / Good to know

Start with a few answers.

Do you offer annual cleaning contracts?
Yes. This website is for businesses seeking recurring commercial cleaning under an annual agreement. Your specific scope, schedule and terms are agreed before service begins.
How often can my property be cleaned?
Tell us the frequency and times you have in mind, or ask for guidance. The right schedule depends on your property, the cleaning tasks and availability, and is confirmed in your quote and agreement.
What areas do you serve?
A1 serves Lethbridge, Coaldale and Coalhurst. Include your property’s location in your enquiry so we can confirm availability and discuss a suitable cleaning schedule.
How do I get an accurate quote?
Start with your property type, location and recurring cleaning needs. After discussing your requirements and arranging a site visit, we can define a service list and written quote. Prices depend on the agreed work.
Does an enquiry book a cleaning service?
No. An enquiry begins a discussion. Services and a start date are confirmed only after the scope, quote, annual agreement and scheduling have been settled.
What should I include in my enquiry?
Your business name, contact information, city, property type and preferred frequency are a helpful start. Approximate floor area and preferred times are optional. Please don’t include access codes, keys or other security information.
